A site to allow you to browse train timetables of countries around the world, as long as they provide open data.

Data may be provided by the infrastructure operator, or passenger train operator(s). In the former case they will be placed in a namespace referencing the primary country in which they operate infrastructure. In the latter case they will be placed in a namespace referencing the country in which the majority of their operations run; if they run a majority of international trains, they will instead be placed in zz to indicate this.
